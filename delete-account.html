Delete your account and data

75hrd is offered by SundayTech Private Limited. You can delete your anonymous account and its associated data in the app without contacting us.

Delete in the app

  1. Open 75hrd on the phone where you used it.
  2. Go to You and tap Delete account and all data.
  3. Confirm Delete everything. Keep the app installed and connected until it confirms removal. If verification fails, use Retry deletion or contact us.

This removes your program profile, exercise progress, check-ins and any discussion data from that phone and our server. Deletion is permanent. It does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription; cancel that separately in your store account.

Request deletion or help

If you cannot use the in-app control, email sundaytechventures@gmail.com with the subject 75hrd account deletion request. Tell us that you want your 75hrd account and data deleted, and whether you still have access to the phone that used the app. Do not send health answers, payment details or your device secret by email. The account is anonymous, so an email address alone may not identify it; we will reply with a safe way to verify which account is yours. We will confirm when the request is complete or explain what more is needed.

Data covered

Deletion covers the anonymous profile (age range, goal, reminder and start date), exercise history, weekly check-ins, invite data and any discussion posts, replies, blocks or reports. We do not collect a name or email to create the account. Content someone else copied outside the app cannot be recalled. Store purchase records are controlled by Google or Apple and are not removed by deleting a 75hrd account.
