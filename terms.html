What 75hrd is

A 75-day program of pelvic-floor, breathing and mobility exercises plus health education for adults 18 and over. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace a doctor or physiotherapist.

Use it safely

  • Stop any exercise that causes pain, and talk to a clinician about symptoms that last, get worse or worry you.
  • Get urgent care for chest pain, pressure or breathlessness during sex or exercise, an erection that is painful or lasts more than 4 hours, a new injury or severe pelvic pain, or sudden weakness, numbness or trouble speaking.
  • If you have had recent pelvic, prostate or abdominal surgery, or live with pelvic pain, check with your clinician before starting.
